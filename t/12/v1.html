The Pattern

You don't lose money at the point of failure.
You lose it in the silence before anyone notices.

Inside Your Engineering Systems

A workflow stalls in Teamcenter and nobody owns it. A migration drags because the team's stretched thin. An ECR sits in review three weeks longer than it should, waiting on a handoff nobody formally owns. Six months later it's not a line item — it's a program delay everyone's asking about.

On Your Production Floor

A step gets missed mid-shift. A part goes out of spec by a margin too small for a human eye on a fixed schedule to reliably catch. Nobody flags it — there was nothing there to flag it. Weeks later it's not a process note — it's a rejection call from your biggest customer, and an RCA that takes longer than the fix.

Different floor. Same failure mode: a process everyone assumed was being followed, and no reliable way to know if it actually was — until it already cost you.
