FAQ

Questions worth answering upfront.

Do you require visa sponsorship or relocation?

No. Every engagement is Corp-to-Corp — no W-2 employment and no sponsorship required. I'm not pursuing on-site-only placements or roles that require U.S. citizenship or permanent residency.

Where are you based, and how does remote delivery work?

100% remote from India, with daily schedule overlap into European business hours and a solid working window across U.S. Eastern, Central, and Pacific time. Onsite visits for milestones like kickoff or go-live can be arranged by prior agreement, travel and visa status permitting.

What Teamcenter versions and platforms do you support?

Teamcenter Unified Architecture from v9 through current cloud releases, plus Active Workspace, Teamcenter Engineering, and Teamcenter Enterprise. I also have hands-on experience with Windchill and eMatrix.

How do engagements typically start?

With a 30-minute discovery call to understand your environment and goals, followed by a short written scope and proposal. No lengthy procurement process is needed just to have the conversation.

Do you take on government or defense-cleared work?

No — I'm not pursuing government or defense-clearance engagements. My focus is manufacturing and technology organizations in the commercial sector.

Can you embed with our team, or is this project-only?

Both. Embedded team augmentation is one of three core engagement models, built for long-term integration into your engineering or IT team — not just isolated project drops.
