The Pattern We Keep Seeing

Different symptom. Same root cause.

Ask an engineering manager and a plant head about their worst week and you'll hear two different stories. Underneath, it's usually the same failure: information that doesn't move fast or cleanly enough between the people who design a product and the people who build it.

01 — ENGINEER

The system of record slows down

Change requests queue up behind a stretched team. Configuration drifts release to release. Nobody fully trusts the BOM anymore.

→
02 — HAND OVER

Intent doesn't survive the handover

This connective layer is sometimes called the digital thread. When it's thin or missing, engineering and production quietly pay for it separately.

→
03 — PRODUCE

Process discipline breaks down

SOPs get skipped under pressure. Deviations aren't caught until the customer catches them.

The cost shows up as the same three numbers, wherever the break happened: longer cycle times, more engineering and manufacturing changes, and higher rejection rates. GompaTech works at both ends of this chain — and treats the handover between them as part of the job, not somebody else's problem.

About GompaTech

A boutique practice, built on both ends of the gap.

GompaTech is a boutique engineering consultancy founded on a simple observation: the biggest losses in manufacturing businesses rarely come from one bad decision. They come from the gap — where engineering data and shop-floor reality quietly drift apart.

We're built by engineers who've worked both sides of that gap: 15+ years of enterprise PLM delivery inside organizations including Google, Boeing, and T.D. Williamson, paired with hands-on process engineering on Indian foundry floors. That combination is unusual, and it's why our Teamcenter work stays grounded in shop-floor reality, and our shop-floor systems stay disciplined about data.

We stay intentionally small and senior — every engagement is led by the people who delivered the last one, not handed down a bench. One team, accountable across the full loop, not two vendors you have to coordinate yourself.
